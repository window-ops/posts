What is Human Nature?
Human nature is a complex concept. While we may not fully understand the nature of human beings, one thing is certain: our inherent ability to survive has shaped us in profound ways.

The idea of human nature tends to be invoked when discussing both our potential and our limitations. It's often used as an excuse for inaction, denying the ideas that could improve quality of life significantly. Restorative justice, a concept aimed at repairing harm rather than punishing offenders, is a prime example.

While human nature may have played a role in shaping our survival instincts, it doesn't necessarily dictate our behavior or limit our capacity for positive change. It is not inherently selfish or destructive, as some might suggest. Rather, it is a complex and multifaceted force that can be harnessed for good.

Therefore, instead of using human nature as an excuse for inaction, we should recognize its potential and use it as an impetus for positive transformation. By understanding our inherent strengths and weaknesses, we can create a more just, equitable, and compassionate society for all.
