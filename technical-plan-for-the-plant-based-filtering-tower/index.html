Technical Plan for the Plant-based Filtering Tower (fictional)
The Plant-based Filtering Tower (PFT) is designed to reduce squalor in cities by utilizing plant-based filtration to absorb pollutants, reduce heat, and treat sewage passively. This innovative tower structure will be built in central urban areas to improve environmental quality and enhance urban living conditions.

Design Components:
  • Central Shaft: Contains a staircase, water pipeline, sewage pipeline, and nutrient pipeline to facilitate movement and essential resource distribution throughout the tower.
  • Multiple Floors: Connected to the central shaft, each floor will be covered with dirt and equipped with water and nutrient pipelines for plant growth.
  • Planting Seeds: Carefully selected seeds with excellent Carbon Capture and Storage (CCS) properties will be planted on each floor to maximize air purification and pollutant absorption.
  • Exposure to Light: The tower's design ensures that the floors are exposed to natural light and the external environment to support plant growth and overall tower functionality.
Functionality:
  • Pollutant Absorption: The tower's plant-covered floors will actively absorb pollutants from the air, contributing to improved air quality in the surrounding urban area.
  • Heat Reduction: The plant-based filtration system will help reduce heat in the city by providing shade and promoting natural cooling mechanisms.
  • Sewage Treatment: Through the use of nutrient-rich water and soil, the tower will passively treat some of the city's sewage, promoting a sustainable and eco-friendly approach to waste management.
  • Low Energy Usage: The design of the tower emphasizes passive processes, minimizing the need for active machinery and reducing energy consumption.
Additional Features:
  • Parks: Utilizing some upper and lower levels of the tower as parks will provide green spaces for recreation and relaxation within the urban environment.
  • High-density Plant Formation: Most levels of the tower will remain untouched to allow for a dense and diverse plant ecosystem, maximizing the tower's environmental benefits.
