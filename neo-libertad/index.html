Neo-Libertad (fictional)
Welcome to Neo-Libertad, a city where corruption thrives among its government officials, all too eager to help the wealthy elites line their pockets. The campaign promise of tax cuts trickling down to the masses has instead resulted in an unprecedented divides between the rich and poor.

Unchecked corporate polluters destroy the environment, leading to catastrophic wildfires, destructive flooding, deadly locust swarms, and misleading information about environmental issues. Amidst these escalating crises, the leaders make extravagant getaways, instead of fixing the turmoil they've caused.

Greedy corporations recklessly dump toxic waste into the air and waters, then point fingers at regular folks for harming the planet. Nutrient-rich food becomes unaffordable for most, pushing people towards consuming low-cost, ultra-processed meals daily. Only the privileged few can enjoy healthy diets and longer lives.

Hospitals suffer neglect under this repressive system, dealing with faulty machinery, widespread mold, and overwhelming patient loads. Rapid disease transmission ensues due to poor conditions, affecting primarily those unable to afford astronomical healthcare costs set by exclusive private facilities catering only to the wealthy.

Neo-Libertad reveals itself as a land of contrasts—one of privilege and decadence for the high society vs. hardship and desperation for the struggling majority. Meager welfare exists merely to appease the rich, while workers face cutthroat capitalism alone.

Brave souls advocating reform are suppressed, punished, or violently confronted by those entrusted to uphold law and order. Fear keeps dissidents quiet and obedient.

This once-hopeful country now serves as a breeding ground for monopolistic companies, ruling every corner of life inside Neo-Libertad's boundaries. Basic necessities like electricity and emergency response teams fall under their control, removing any independence or progress beyond the grasp of corporate power.

Neo-Libertad stands as a cautionary tale against high levels of corruption and financial gaps left unaddressed: A chilling reminder of the consequences when governments put self-interest before public service.
