Classification
Special Containment Procedures
Special Containment Procedures: SCP-[...] is to be monitored remotely by Foundation agents embedded within the European Union (EU) Parliament. These agents are to maintain positions in various member states' governments and political parties, with a particular focus on those affiliated with the political group that SCP-[...] is part of. The primary objective is to track the emergence and activities of SCP-[...] and ensure its containment in its dormant state or use in its active state.

Should SCP-[...] become active, the Foundation's agents are to discreetly facilitate its presence in at least five (5) member states of the EU. This will initiate a containment protocol wherein SCP-[...]'s influence will counteract and suppress far-right political groups within the EU Parliament.
Description
SCP-[...] is an anomalous political party that manifests within a European large political group with left-wing and communist ideologies. SCP-[...] presents itself as an extreme left-wing faction, advocating for radical political and economic reforms. Despite its seemingly disruptive nature, SCP-[...]'s primary effect is to counteract and neutralize far-right political groups, particularly those with extremist tendencies.

When SCP-[...] becomes active and establishes a presence in at least five (5) member states of the EU, it triggers a series of events that lead to a significant shift in the European Parliament's composition. Through unknown means, SCP-[...]'s influence causes a rapid decline in the popularity and support for far-right parties, effectively preventing their rise to power. Simultaneously, centrist and moderate political groups also experience a decrease in popularity, allowing various radical left-wing parties to gain a majority in the EU Parliament.

This anomalous shift in political power results in a temporary rule of the EU by SCP-[...] and its affiliated parties. During this period, SCP-[...]'s influence leads to the implementation of numerous policies and reforms, often with positive societal effects, particularly for marginalized communities. However, SCP-[...]'s presence is transient, and after a variable period, it suddenly disappears. Following its disappearance, the EU Parliament returns to its original state, with political power distributed among various parties, including centrist and moderate groups, as if SCP-[...]'s influence had never occurred.
Addendum A: Interview with Dr. █████, Political Anomalies Specialist
Interviewer: Dr. █████, you've been studying SCP-[...] for several months now. What can you tell us about its origin and nature?

Dr. █████: SCP-[...] is a fascinating anomaly, one that challenges our understanding of political dynamics. From our research, it seems SCP-[...] is a self-correcting mechanism of sorts. It emerges in response to the growing threat of far-right extremism within the EU. By temporarily tipping the scales towards the radical left, it effectively neutralizes the far-right's influence and prevents potential catastrophic consequences.

Interviewer: But why the need for such an extreme measure? Why not just contain the far-right groups directly?

Dr. █████: That's the intriguing part. Our containment procedures have been successful in preventing the rise of far-right extremism in the EU so far. But SCP-[...] suggests there's a deeper, underlying issue. It's almost as if the anomaly is a failsafe, ensuring the EU's political landscape remains balanced, even if it means temporarily swinging the pendulum to the other extreme.

Interviewer: So, what's our next step in understanding and managing SCP-[...]?

Dr. █████: We're currently analyzing historical data and SCP-[...]'s effects to identify potential triggers. We must also be prepared to discreetly intervene if SCP-[...]'s conditions are met again. It's a delicate task, as we don't want to disrupt the natural political processes, but we must ensure the EU remains stable and that SCP-[...] doesn't cause unintended consequences.
