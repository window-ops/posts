A Fairer Taxation System
Tax cuts for the rich are irresponsible. They benefit those who already possess significant wealth and do not stimulate economic growth.

Instead of cutting taxes for the wealthy, we should focus on implementing a fairer tax system that includes:
  • Raising Taxes on the Rich: Higher taxes on the wealthiest individuals can provide more funds for essential services such as healthcare, education, and infrastructure.
  • Restricting Offshore Capital Movement: By restricting the movement of capital to offshore accounts, we will help close the loopholes that allow the rich to evade taxes.
  • Global Tax Agency Collaboration: Uniting tax agencies worldwide would create a more cohesive system that prevents tax evasion and ensures compliance.
  • Mandatory Income Disclosure: Requiring full income disclosure without deductions would eliminate the complexities that allow for tax avoidance.
Opponents of fair taxation often argue that closing loopholes will drive the rich to hide their money in black markets. However, this perspective ignores the reality that a well-regulated and transparent tax system can deter such behavior.

A Resource-Based Economy (RBE) can be an important framework for rectifying the flaws of our current monetary system, with the absence of money, taxes and classes allowing for a more equitable distribution of resources. However, before we can transition to such a system, we must first address taxation and economic inequality.

In conclusion, by raising taxes on the wealthy, closing loopholes, and ensuring transparency, we can create an economy that serves everyone. It is time to reject austerity politics and invest in the future of our communities.
