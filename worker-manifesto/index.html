Worker Manifesto
People shall be able to determine their own temporalities. No structure, whether social or economic, shall coerce the people into organizing time in the name of "efficiency" or "profits".

People shall be free when work ends; that is the company or cooperative shall not be allowed to impose any form of overtime work, especially if its unpaid or exhausting, taking away time from personal matters.

When people are sick, all leaves shall be paid, and all medical treatment must be free and universal, and of high quality.

Capitalism is inherently oppressive and alienating, and all of its structures shall be gradually replaced with more equitable and ecological structures.

The right to protest and unionize are guaranteed, and except situations that may escalate into violence, they shall be protected.

The minimum wage shall be the living wage, or wages shall be irrelevant and resources distributed according to needs.
