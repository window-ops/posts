Why Universal, Free Healthcare is a Necessity
When we become ill, we require immediate access to quality medical care. However, for many people, it is prohibitively expensive to receive the necessary treatment. Medical debt increases financial stress and anxiety, which can worsen health issues.

This is why universal, free healthcare is necessary. And it should not just be about giving basic medical care, but also socializing all medical facilities and making all forms of treatment, not just the most basic ones, free and accessible to everyone.

The Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR) recognizes healthcare as a fundamental human right, stating in Article 25 that "everyone has the right to a standard of living adequate for the health and well-being of himself and of his family, including food, clothing, housing and medical care and necessary social services, and the right to security in the event of unemployment, sickness, disability, widowhood, old age or other lack of livelihood in circumstances beyond his control". Private healthcare systems prioritize profits over people, violating this fundamental right and contribute to our society's health disparities.

It is time to realize that healthcare is a human right, not a commodity. Implementing universal, free healthcare not only improves people's health and well-being, but also contributes to a more just and equitable society.
