Classification
Special Containment Procedures
Special Containment Procedures: SCP-[...] is to be contained within Bio-Research Site-██, a facility equipped to handle biological anomalies. Due to the nature of SCP-[...]'s transmission, containment efforts focus on minimizing its spread and providing treatment to affected individuals.

All instances of SCP-[...]-HZIV and SCP-[...]-CL are to be stored in secure biological containment units within Site-██. Samples are to be handled by personnel wearing Level B hazmat suits to prevent accidental exposure.

Individuals infected with SCP-[...] are to be identified and tracked by Foundation agents embedded in government welfare agencies in affected regions, primarily Germany and Australia. When a new case is identified, the subject is to be discreetly offered assistance by Foundation front organizations, providing temporary accommodation and financial support to ensure their basic needs are met. This support is to be provided until the subject's symptoms cease, indicating the virus has left their body.

In the event of an outbreak, mobile containment teams are to be deployed to the affected area to administer amnestics and provide cover stories as necessary. Foundation agents within local media outlets will ensure that reports of SCP-[...]'s effects are suppressed.
Description
SCP-[...] is an anomalous virus that primarily affects individuals receiving government welfare benefits in regions with workfare or limited welfare programs. The virus manifests in two main variants, SCP-[...]-HZIV and SCP-[...]-CL, primarily affecting Germany and Australia, respectively.

Infected individuals experience auditory hallucinations in the form of a voice repeating the phrase, "Get off welfare, get into work" and/or "It's not enough for you", whenever they receive a welfare payment. The voice has not been identified and in considered androgynous. The phrase is repeated an undetermined number of times, causing varying degrees of psychological distress.

SCP-[...] vacates the host's body if the infected individual ceases to receive welfare payments for a continuous period of one week. This process appears to be the virus' method of transmission, as it seeks new hosts who are reliant on welfare.

Addendum B: Research Experiment Log - SCP-[...]-HZIV/CL Combination
<Start of Log>

Date: [Redacted]
Researchers: Dr. █████, Dr. ████, and Research Assistant ████

Procedure: In an attempt to understand the potential effects of combining SCP-[...]-HZIV and SCP-[...]-CL, a controlled experiment was conducted within Bio-Research Site-██'s containment chamber. A sample of SCP-[...]-CL was introduced to a contained instance of SCP-[...]-HZIV, and the resulting interaction was observed.

Results: Upon contact, the two viral strains exhibited a unique reaction. The SCP-[...]-CL strain appeared to 'overwrite' the SCP-[...]-HZIV strain, resulting in a new, hybrid virus. This hybrid virus, designated SCP-[...]-HYB, displayed characteristics of both parent strains but with heightened anomalous properties.

Infected subjects exposed to SCP-[...]-HYB experienced auditory hallucinations similar to those caused by the original strains, but with increased intensity and frequency. The voices create a disturbing, multilingual chorus. The phrase "Your reliance is a burden, work to contribute" was repeatedly uttered, causing severe distress to the subjects.

Furthermore, SCP-[...]-HYB demonstrated an increased transmission rate. Unlike the parent strains, which vacate the host after a week of non-receipt of welfare, SCP-[...]-HYB remains in the host's body indefinitely, continuously seeking new hosts through close contact. This makes containment and treatment significantly more challenging.

Conclusion: The combination of SCP-[...]-HZIV and SCP-[...]-CL results in a more aggressive and contagious anomalous virus. Further research is required to develop effective countermeasures and containment protocols for SCP-[...].

<End of Log>
Addendum D: Origin Hypotheses for SCP-[...] Strains
SCP-[...]-CL: The SCP-[...]-CL strain is believed to have originated in Canberra, Australia, during a political stunt by an unknown member of the opposition party in the lower house. The incident involved a prop paycheck, intended to mock the government's handling of welfare payments. It is hypothesized that the anomalous virus was inadvertently created and released during this event, affecting nearby individuals who were on Centrelink benefits.

SCP-[...]-HZIV: The HZ variant of SCP-[...] emerged shortly after the approval of Agenda 2010, specifically the Hartz IV reforms, in the Bundestag, Germany. The strain's emergence is thought to be linked to the intense political debate and public outcry surrounding these welfare reforms. The emotional and psychological stress of the affected population may have contributed to the manifestation of this anomalous virus.

While the exact origins of SCP-[...] remain unclear, these hypotheses provide potential insights into the virus's initial appearances and its connection to significant welfare policy changes in the affected regions. Further research and investigation are ongoing to uncover more details about SCP-[...]'s origins and its unusual response to welfare-related societal factors.
