The Neoliberal Declaration (fictional)
We, the corporate beneficiaries of the neoliberal agenda,

In this magnificent theater of capitalism, where the invisible hand of the market gently caresses our wallets, we come together to express our passionate sympathy with our corporate donors.

Whereas we acknowledge that the genuine essence of democracy lies not in the voices of the people, but in the whispers of lobbyists and the generous contributions of those who truly matter—our respected beneficiaries.

Whereas the misery of the average citizen is just a background noise in the symphony of stock prices and quarterly profits reports, we are united in our determination to prioritize the interests of the few over the needs of the many.

Whereas, we understand that the term "free market" is a euphemism for the freedom to exploit, and that deregulation is simply a means of ensuring that our corporate cronies may thrive without the limitations of ethics or accountability.

Let it be resolved that we will continue to advocate for policies that favor tax cuts for billionaires while providing the people with the pleasant sense of choice in a marketplace that is far from free.

Finally, let us raise our eyes to the magnificent future of neoliberalism, in which the suffering of the people will be forgotten by the profits of the wealthy. Who needs social safety nets when we've got golden parachutes?

Signed: A Concerned Corporate Executive (and staunch capitalist).
