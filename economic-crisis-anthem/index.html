Economic Crisis Anthem
When the skies turn gray and the markets fall,
Neoliberals stand up, they’re ready to call:
“Tax cuts for the rich!” they shout with glee!
“Deregulate the market! Let it run free!”

In times of crisis, they know what to say!
“Sell out the government!”
“Bail out the banks!”
“Encourage speculation!”

“Massive austerity packs!” they cheer from the stage,
“Call the IMF!”
“Cut welfare!”
“Make trickle-down economics work!”

Oh, in times of crisis, they know what to say!
“Make 2008 great again!”
“Socialism for the rich and Capitalism for the poor!”
While the rest of us struggle to make ends meet.

In times of crisis, we’ll change what they say!
No more “Tax cuts for the rich!”
We’ll build a new future where all can thrive,
No more trickle-down economics!
