The Universal Basic Needs programme (fictional) / Eligibility Requirements
To ensure the effective and ethical delivery of these services, UBN has a set of requirements for individuals and businesses seeking to benefit from or partner with the program. These requirements are essential to protect the integrity of the program, safeguard against potential abuses, and ensure UBN's resources are directed towards those who need them most.

Individual Requirements:
  • Valid Identification and Residency: All individuals must provide valid proof of identification, such as a government-issued ID card, passport, or equivalent, to verify their identity and current residence. This information is necessary for UBN to tailor its services to the specific needs of each individual and ensure efficient delivery.
  • Age Requirement: UBN services are available to individuals aged 16 and above. This age restriction is in place to manage risk and prevent potential misuse of UBN resources. Younger individuals below this age can still benefit from UBN by registering under the guidance and consent of a parent or legal guardian.
  • Country of Residence: UBN aims to reach as many people as possible, but it is important to note that the program's services are subject to the laws and regulations of each country. UBN can only operate within countries that allow its presence and services, and individuals must reside in these reachable areas to be eligible for assistance.
  • Security and Welfare Considerations: UBN is committed to ensuring the safety and well-being of all its beneficiaries and partner organizations. Therefore, individuals who pose a security risk to any populace, welfare organization, or climate action group are ineligible to receive UBN services. This includes individuals with a history of violent behavior, involvement in activities detrimental to the environment, or those actively working against the welfare of communities.
Business Requirements:

For businesses seeking to partner with or receive support from UBN, additional requirements must be met to ensure alignment with UBN's values and mission:
  • No Lobbying: Lobbying activities aimed at influencing government policies or decisions for financial gain are strictly prohibited. UBN considers lobbying a form of corruption that often hinders much-needed progress in climate and welfare action. Any business found to have engaged in lobbying within the past year will be ineligible for UBN support.
  • No Greenwashing: Businesses must not engage in misleading marketing or advertising that overstates their environmental benefits. We prioritize transparency and honest communication about climate action. Any business found greenwashing within the last year will be ineligible for UBN support.
  • Proper Waste Disposal: Businesses must not have improperly dumped waste in developing countries within the last year. This unsustainable and unethical practice is detrimental to the environment and the well-being of vulnerable communities. UBN is committed to promoting sustainable waste management practices and will only partner with businesses that share this commitment.
  • Exclusion of Fossil Fuel and Unhealthy Foods Businesses: Companies primarily engaged in the fossil fuel or unhealthy food industry, are ineligible for UBN support. These businesses pose unacceptable risks, including potential climate change denial, health risks, improper waste disposal, and lobbying against climate action.
  • Acknowledgment of Climate Change: UBN requires businesses to acknowledge the reality and urgency of climate change. Any business found to deny or downplay the existence or impacts of climate change, through public statements or actions, will be ineligible for UBN support.
