The Universal Basic Needs programme (fictional) / The Synthetic Foods Initiative (SFI)
The Synthetic Foods Initiative (SFI) is an initiative made by UBN to mass-produce synthetic foods. It provides nutritious, customizable, and eco-friendly food options to all participants via an exhaustive selection of synthetic ingredients derived from plant-based or animal-free sources.

All energy required to produce the ingredients comes from renewable sources, all disposable materials used for the sterilization process are disinfected and then recycled, all material used to feed the cells come from safe and healthy ingredients that are also used to feed animals in organic farms, the foods produced have a short lifespan due to low to no amounts of conservatives, all cancerous cells that may occur are eliminated.

To cater to varying skill levels and time constraints among users, the SFI has three distinct processing levels:
  • Raw: Ideal for those who enjoy preparing meals from scratch or wish to develop their culinary skills. Most nutrients are preserved.
  • Processed as Culinary Ingredients: Suitable for individuals who prefer semi-prepared components but still desire some involvement in meal preparation.
  • Fully Processed - Designed for busy individuals seeking convenience without ultraprocessing. Some nutrients are lost during the processing.
By utilizing synthetic foods, the SFI significantly diminishes resource consumption compared to conventional agriculture. Consequently, greenhouse gas emissions, deforestation, soil degradation, and water pollution associated with farming activities are substantially reduced. Moreover, delivering food via bikes and drones ensures minimal carbon footprints during transport.

With precise control over synthetic food production, waste generation is virtually eliminated. Each order being prepared according to user specifications along with the obligation to share prevents excess food from being discarded. The packaging used for deliveries is reusable, recyclable and biodegradable.

As part of the platform, individuals can learn about proper meal planning and achieving long-term wellness goals through informed decisions regarding their food choices.

In addition to the AI-powered chatbot available for troubleshooting issues related to account management and ordering processes, the SFI community actively contributes to a shared knowledge base. Participants can engage in discussions, share recipes, provide feedback, and suggest improvements, creating collaboration and continuous enhancement within the platform.

A guide to obtaining the benefits of UBN's SFI

Creating an Account:
  1. Visit the Website: Go to sfi.ubn.org using your preferred web browser.
  2. Sign Up: Click on the 'Create Account' or 'Sign Up' button, usually located at the top right corner of the homepage.
  3. Enter Details: Fill in the required fields with your personal information, such as your name, email address, and password.
  4. Provide Address: Enter your delivery address where you would like your food packages to be delivered.
  5. Verify Account: Check your email for a verification link and click on it to activate your account.
Adding and Personalizing Food Packages:
  1. Log In: Access your SFI dashboard by entering your credentials on the login page.
  2. Add a Package: Click on the plus icon to add a new food package.
  3. Set Delivery Frequency: Choose how often you want your food delivered—weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
  4. Select Ingredients: Drag and drop raw ingredients into your package from the extensive list provided. You can choose from various synthetic meats, grains, vegetables, and more.
  5. Customize Processing Level: Select the level of processing for your ingredients — 'Raw', 'Processed as Culinary Ingredients', or 'Fully Processed'.
  6. Adjust Ratios: Decide on the ratio between crops and meats according to your dietary preferences.
  7. Save Your Package: Once you're satisfied with your selections, click 'Save' to finalize your package.
Remember, the food is produced in a carbon-neutral facility, and delivery is designed to be quick and environmentally friendly. Enjoy cooking with your personalized ingredients!
