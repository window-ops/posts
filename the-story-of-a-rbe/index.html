The story of a RBE (fictional)
Phase 1: The Beginning

As the world deals with the consequences of global temperatures rising 2 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels, ecosystems are rapidly deteriorating, highlighting the need for economic change. Even in the face of efforts to reduce pollution, it is becoming clear that the fundamental problem is a system focused on endless growth, regardless of consequences.

Developed through an international collaboration of scientists, engineers and programmers, an advanced artificial intelligence known as the Automated World Management Module (AWMM) promises to revolutionize resource management, eradicate poverty and prioritize sustainability.

In the 2080s, amid economic instability and social unrest, revolutionaries take control and usher a new era of governance with a resource-based economy empowered by the AWMM. With the old system dismantled, humanity embarks on a mission to rebalance nature and reverse the effects of climate change.

A Resource-Based Economy (RBE) is an economic model that prioritizes the effective and sustainable utilization of natural resources, leveraging technological advancements and scientific principles to cater to the necessities of every individual. The management of resources in an RBE adheres to rigorous scientific standards aimed at enhancing efficiency while reducing wastage. It incorporates sophisticated technologies like automation and artificial intelligence to streamline production and distribution channels.

It employs systems thinking to comprehend the intricate web of connections between various components of the economy and societal structures. By doing so, it facilitates the discovery of comprehensive remedies and averts unintended consequences. Furthermore, this model encourages the adoption of renewable and reusable resources alongside reduced energy and material usage.

Decision-making follows the scientific methodology, ensuring choices are predicated on empirical evidence instead of subjective or partisan inclinations. Consequently, judgements become more fact-driven to minimize any potential harm.

Replacing monetary transactions and market forces, a RBE implements a demand-driven allocation system. This mechanism guarantees equitable access to essential resources for leading a comfortable lifestyle. An international network of interlinked computers governs this process, accurately tracking, managing, and coordinating the generation and dissemination of commodities and services based on genuine requirements. In other words, resources are distributed to each according to their needs instead of market dynamics.

Phase 2: Restoring Nature

Fast forward to 2100, a wave of migration to newly established smart cities signals a shift towards sustainable living. These metropolises integrate harmoniously with the AWMM, ensuring an efficient distribution of resources and significantly improving quality of life. Guided by scientific principles and supported by automation, basic needs are easily met, allowing individuals to pursue their passions and contribute to ecological restoration efforts.

With nature regaining its glory and society embracing a culture of innovation and cooperation, the younger generation is emerging as leaders of change. Driven by purpose and passion, they are leading humanity towards a future where climate goals become achievable.

Phase 3: Achieving Climate Goals

By 2500, the world is witnessing an incredible transformation. Nature flourishes, technology advances exponentially, and the AWMM demonstrates its supreme effectiveness in managing resources. With climate goals achieved, society enjoys unprecedented prosperity and technological progress.

In retrospect, without the progressive initiatives of the resource-based economy, the transformative capabilities of the AWMM and the dedication to restoring the natural balance, the fate of society would have been drastically different at the beginning of the 22nd century.
