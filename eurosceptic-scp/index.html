Classification
Special Containment Procedures
SCP-[...]-J is to be contained by maintaining a consistent narrative of European unity, economic prosperity, and unwavering commitment to human rights. This is to be achieved through:
  • Extensive Media Control: All major news outlets within the European Union are to be subtly influenced to promote positive stories about the EU and downplay any negative events (especially those concerning trade deals, border control, or internal dissent).
  • Targeted Propaganda Campaigns: Regular public awareness campaigns emphasizing the benefits of EU membership (e.g., freedom of movement, access to the single market) are to be launched. Slogans like "United in Diversity" and "Stronger Together" are to be disseminated widely.
  • Strategic Partnerships with Academia: Research grants and funding are to be directed towards academics who produce favorable studies on the EU's impact. Dissident voices are to be subtly discredited or ignored.
  • Swift Response to Dissent: Any individual or organization that attempts to disseminate information contrary to the established narrative is to be swiftly and decisively countered. This may involve public shaming, accusations of "Russian interference," or legal action.
  • "Fact-Checking" Initiatives: Partnering with friendly fact-checking organizations to swiftly debunk "misinformation" related to the EU. Any criticism of the EU, no matter how valid, must be labeled as false or misleading.
Description
SCP-[...]-J designates a complex series of memetic hazards that coalesce into the perception of the European Union as a fundamentally flawed and potentially imperialistic entity. The effects of SCP-[...]-J exposure vary in intensity, but invariably involve a degradation of perceived loyalty towards the European Union, and are as follows:
  • Increased skepticism regarding the official narrative of the EU project.
  • Increased propensity to support political parties and movements identified as "Progressive" and/or Eurosceptic".
  • Active opposition to policies enacted by the European Union, often expressed through public demonstrations, online activism, and/or the creation and dissemination of counter-narratives.
  • Dissemination of information critical of the European Union, often labeled as "anti-EU propaganda" by containment personnel.
  • Formulation of alternative models for European cooperation, frequently characterized by a desire for reduced centralization of power and increased national sovereignty.
The primary vectors for SCP-[...]-J are:
  • Exposure to factual information and/or critical analysis pertaining to the European Union's trade agreements with developing nations, particularly regarding allegations of exploitation or unfair practices.
  • Direct or indirect observation of the activities of Frontex (the European Border and Coast Guard Agency) at the European Union's external borders, particularly incidents involving the treatment of migrants and asylum seekers.
  • Exposure to critical analyses of the European Union originating from sources identified as leftist or nationalist, regardless of their factual accuracy.
  • Engaging in prolonged and/or critical thought regarding the long-term implications of European Union policies and governance.
