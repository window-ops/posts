On Authoritarian Self-Description

Even if I had deep knowledge in sociology and politics, I'd still be unfit for power. Handing power to any one person is dangerous, as you never know what even a well-intentioned individual might do to society. Power belongs in collective deliberation, not lone hands.

I support democracy and dream of a non-repressive reality principle. But my craving for harmony holds me back, since harmony often demands destruction or control, as Herbert Marcuse theorized in "Eros and Civilization". My increasingly "authoritarian" personality stems from that dynamic; the Nirvana desire, Freud's pull toward a frictionless void via the death drive, that compulsion to return to inorganic stillness through aggressive negation. As a rationalist, I know imposing an oppressive social order defies reason. I don't crave oppression; it's the urge to control the younger generation that pulls me there.

Yet threats like climate change demand deliberate action. Eco-socialists rightly push interventions to curb damage; deep ecology does the same. I share eco-socialism's premises, so "authoritarianism" gets complicated: any democratic socialist wants to steer society toward their vision. Economic democracy benefits workers (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rSc6OqSPq2E), so production and economic activities should be organized around worker ownership. Conflicts primarily erupt in the social realm (such as the differences between Zizek and Adorno), and that in part contributes to my "authoritarian" self-perception.

We can't force a single socialist order on everyone. We can't convince the entire young generation ditch mindless TikTok scrolling. We cannot crusade against Windows 11 bloatware, teach Linux at a massive scale, and stop users from ditching obsolete Macs. Preinstalling AntiX on e-waste laptops for poor kids could result in some of them wrecking USB drives because ROX-Filer (the lightweight file manager) automounts drives unsafely and demands unmounting every partition before ejection. We'd bear the blame despite having given them guides and passwords. Ubuntu sits at less than ~4% desktop share and it won't hit 25%, maybe scrape 10% if Microsoft keeps enshittifying Windows under Nadella. We cannot persuade conservative elders that homosexuality occurs across many different species.

As interveners in economic and social life, we carry massive responsibility. We should ease up on control, support deliberation and honor emotions in our thinking, even as rationalists. Ignoring them endangers our health.
