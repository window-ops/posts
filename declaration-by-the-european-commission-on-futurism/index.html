Declaration by the European Commission on Futurism (fictional)
Today, the European Commission proudly announces the official adoption of European Futurism as the defining style of our governance, communication, and cultural identity. It embodies a visionary aesthetic that fuses grand names, forward-looking narratives, and an unwavering commitment to progress.

The Hallmarks of European Futurism: Grandiosity Meets Pragmatism

Our programs and policies now proudly bear names as evocative and futuristic as the ambitions they symbolize. Among these masterpieces are:
  • NextGenerationEU: Because Europe always deserves a "next generation", endlessly renewed and rebooted.
  • Horizon Europe: Gazing into infinite frontiers of science and innovation, as limitless as the name suggests.
  • Digital Compass 2030: Guiding us unwaveringly into a brave new digital dawn.
  • Single Market 2.0: An upgraded internal market that embraces the language of software releases, proving we are forever modern.
  • Innovation Fund: Fueling the engine of European ingenuity with a powerfully simple name that promises the future today.
  • Fit for 55 Package: Strength and fitness metaphors to convince us that cutting emissions is as achievable as a workout goal.
  • Digital Europe Programme: Because Europe deserves a digital destiny worthy of a title.
  • InvestEU Programme: Investing not just money, but hope and trust in the European project.
What Does This Mean in Practice?

European Futurism means that every minor policy tweak or reform is wrapped in a cloak of visionary language that inspires confidence, unity, and a sense of inevitable progress. When we say "green and digital", we mean carefully replacing some outdated technologies with slightly newer versions - enough to call it a revolution but mild enough to keep markets stable.

When we promise stability and growth, we mean rescuing companies that stumble while reassuring citizens that the grand narrative of prosperity continues undisturbed. This is governance as performance art - subtle changes elevated to epic sagas.

Closing Statement

With this type of Futurism as our guiding style, the EU steps boldly into a future where every reform is a renaissance, every program a symphony, and every generation is the "next" next generation. Together, we will script a saga that is as grand in name as it is measured in impact.
