Make Informed Decisions: Rely on Data and Science, Not Politics!
Data and science provide us with an objective understanding of the world, allowing us to measure the impact of our actions, learn from our successes and failures, and find the best solutions to the world's most pressing issues, including poverty, disease, climate change, and more. To achieve this, we must collect, share, and utilize high-quality data on all aspects of human and natural systems, and apply the scientific method to test our hypotheses, analyze our data, and communicate our findings. Let's prioritize data and science and make informed decisions for a better future.
