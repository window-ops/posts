The Separation of Corporation and State
The concentration of wealth and power in the hands of a few corporations has led to widespread inequality, environmental degradation, and social injustice. It's time to acknowledge that this is not only undemocratic, but also fundamentally unjust.

This is why the separation of corporation and state is equally important as the separation of church and state, a fundamental principle of liberal democracy.

The separation of corporation and state would mean an end to corporate lobbying, the revolving door, and the notion that corporations are people entitled to the same rights and protections as human beings. It would require a fundamental transformation of our economic system, prioritizing human well-being and the health of the planet over profit and growth.

The separation of corporation and state is not just a moral imperative, but a practical necessity for ensuring that our governments serve the interests of all citizens, not just the powerful and wealthy. Just as we would never tolerate the influence of a single church or religion over our government, we should not tolerate the influence of corporations.
